WHAT WE DO

Specialist Contracting Services

From bulk earthworks to foundation preparation and infrastructure installation. DC Earthworks provides complete enabling packages for developers nationwide.

SERVICE 01

Bulk Earthmoving & Land Modeling

We specialize in bulk muck away, site leveling, cut-and-fill calculations, and material import/export. Utilizing advanced GPS-guided machinery, we execute accurate topsoil strips and subsoil cut-and-fills to ensure exact structural coordinates.

3D GPS Cut & Fill calculations
Bulk material disposal & recycling
Land remediation & capping
Clay liner installations
SERVICE 02

Precision Excavation & Trenching

Our excavators and operators carry out foundation footing trenches, basement digs, retaining walls, attenuation tanks, and water management lagoons. We ensure precise depth profiles in compliance with local structural engineering plans and building regulations.

Strip foundation trenching
Complex basement excavations
Retaining wall preparations
Deep utility service trenches
SERVICE 03

Groundworks & Foundations

From sub-structure blocks and floor slabs to drainage networks and road access construction. We lay the foundations of residential, commercial, and industrial sites. Our capabilities include deep sewers, manhole construction, and attenuation systems.

Ring beam & raft foundations
Concrete slab installations
Deep adoptable drainage (S104)
Access road substructures
SERVICE 04

Heavy Plant Hire (Operated & Self-Drive)

Our modern plant fleet is available for hire on competitive terms. All machines are loaded with Tier 4/V environmentally certified engines and are maintained to standard. Operated hire includes experienced, CPCS-qualified, and safety-audited drivers.

Tracked Excavators (1.5T to 35T)
GPS Intelligent Bulldozers
Dumpers & Rollers
Full attachment options available
SERVICE 05

Civil Engineering & Infrastructure

We provide highway infrastructure works, utilities corridors, deep drainage connections, culverts, and slope-stabilization/retaining systems. Our engineers work in close partnership with structural consultants to deliver durable infrastructure solutions.

Section 278 Highway Works
Retaining structure systems
Precast concrete structural elements
Utility installation packages
SERVICE 06

Site Clearance & Soil Remediation

We prepare greenfield and brownfield sites for construction. Demolition of existing structures, vegetation removal, soil stabilization, and sorting/capping of hazardous materials. We aim to maximize site recycling to prevent high off-site disposal fees.

Demolition of masonry structures
Soil stabilization & lime conditioning
Waste classification & segregation
Crushing & screening of concrete
METHODOLOGY

Our Structured Contracting Process

We follow a rigorous management system to ensure your site is delivered on schedule, fully compliant, and ready for structure handovers.

01

Consultation

Initial review of your structural drawings, site plans, site topography reports, and soil tests.

02

Planning

Preparing 3D cut-and-fill GPS models, producing complete RAMS packs, scheduling mobilization, and signing service SLAs.

03

Execution

Deploying Stage V machinery and qualified CPCS drivers. Site grading and drainage runs managed under engineer supervision.

04

Completion

Final topographic inspections, client walkovers, handover of s104 adoptable documentation, and site demobilization.
